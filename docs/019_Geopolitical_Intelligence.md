# Oil Intelligence Platform — Geopolitical Intelligence System Architecture Document

> **Version:** 1.0
> **Status:** Active
> **Classification:** Core Intelligence Engine

---

## Purpose

This document defines the architecture, methodologies, responsibilities, and operational standards of the Geopolitical Intelligence System within the Oil Intelligence Platform.

The Geopolitical Intelligence System continuously monitors, evaluates, and quantifies geopolitical developments that influence the global oil market. Political events can rapidly alter oil production, transportation, investment, and market sentiment. This system converts those events into structured intelligence that can be consumed by forecasting, risk management, simulation, and strategy engines.

Rather than simply reporting news, the system measures the potential market impact of geopolitical developments through standardized scoring and analytical models.

---

## Mission

The mission of the Geopolitical Intelligence System is:

> To transform geopolitical events into measurable intelligence that improves forecasting accuracy, strengthens risk assessment, and enhances strategic decision-making across the Oil Intelligence Platform.

The Geopolitical Intelligence System answers:

- How will geopolitical events affect oil prices?
- Which regions face increasing instability?
- What is the likelihood of supply disruption?
- Which political developments increase market volatility?
- How should forecasts adjust to emerging geopolitical risks?
- Which geopolitical events require immediate analyst attention?

---

## Core Responsibilities

The Geopolitical Intelligence System is responsible for:

- Evaluating political stability

- Tracking international agreements
- Identifying emerging crises
- Quantifying market impact

## Intelligence Categories

The system should evaluate:

### Armed Conflicts

Monitor:

- Wars
- Military operations
- Border conflicts
- Naval conflicts
- Civil wars
- Terrorist attacks on energy infrastructure

Potential impacts:

- Supply disruption
- Shipping risk
- Insurance costs
- Market volatility

### Sanctions

Track:

- Economic sanctions
- Energy export restrictions
- Financial restrictions
- Trade embargoes
- Secondary sanctions

Evaluate both direct and indirect market impacts.

### OPEC & OPEC+ Decisions

Monitor:

- Production quotas
- Emergency meetings
- Compliance levels
- Strategic announcements
- Output adjustments

These events should receive elevated analytical priority.

### Government Policy

Evaluate:

- Energy legislation
- Environmental regulations
- Export restrictions
- Tax policy
- Strategic petroleum reserve actions
- Subsidies

Policy changes may alter both supply and demand.

### Elections

Monitor elections within major producing and consuming nations.

Assess:

- Policy uncertainty
- Leadership changes
- Energy policy shifts
- Regulatory risk

### Diplomatic Relations

Track:

- Trade agreements
- Diplomatic disputes
- Strategic alliances
- Regional cooperation
- International negotiations

Changes in diplomatic relations can influence energy markets and investment.

## Event Classification

Every event should record:

- Event type
- Geographic region
- Countries involved
- Timestamp
- Source credibility
- Estimated duration
- Expected market impact
- Confidence score

Classification should remain standardized across all event types.

---

## Impact Scoring

Each event should receive quantitative scores for:

- Supply risk
- Demand impact
- Transportation risk
- Infrastructure risk
- Political uncertainty
- Volatility potential
- Overall geopolitical severity

Scores should be normalized for comparison across events.

---

## Historical Repository

Maintain a complete archive of:

- Historical geopolitical events
- Impact scores
- Forecast adjustments
- Market reactions
- Analytical notes
- Validation results

Historical analysis improves future model performance.

---

## Monitoring

Continuously monitor:

- Breaking geopolitical events
- Government announcements
- Diplomatic developments
- Conflict escalation
- Sanctions updates
- Energy policy changes

Monitoring should operate continuously with configurable alert thresholds.

## Security

Protect:

- Intelligence datasets
- Proprietary scoring methodologies
- Historical event archives
- Analyst annotations
- Forecast integrations

Sensitive analytical methods should remain restricted to authorized users.

## Scalability

The system should support:

- Thousands of daily events
- Multi-language news ingestion
- AI-assisted event classification
- Global geographic coverage
- Near real-time analysis
- Continuous model refinement

---

## Anti-Patterns

Avoid:

- Political bias
- Single-source intelligence
- Unverified reporting
- Duplicate event processing
- Hidden scoring methodologies
- Ignoring historical context

---

## Architectural Invariants

The following conditions must always remain true:

1. Every event is traceable to verified sources.
2. Intelligence remains politically neutral.
3. Event scoring is standardized.
4. Historical events remain permanently archived.
5. Forecast adjustments remain explainable.
6. Geopolitical intelligence supports, but does not replace, analyst judgment.

---

## Definition of Compliance

The Geopolitical Intelligence System is compliant when:

1. Global geopolitical developments are continuously monitored.
2. Events are classified using standardized methodologies.
3. Market impacts are quantified through transparent scoring.
4. Historical validation supports forecasting improvements.
5. The platform maintains timely, objective, and actionable geopolitical intelligence.

---

## Final Statement

The Geopolitical Intelligence System provides the political and international affairs intelligence layer of the Oil Intelligence Platform.

By transforming wars, sanctions, government policies, diplomatic developments, elections, OPEC decisions, and regional instability into structured analytical intelligence, the system enables more accurate forecasts, stronger risk management, and better-informed strategic decisions in the global energy market.
