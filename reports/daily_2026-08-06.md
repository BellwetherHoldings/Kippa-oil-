## 4. The Economic & Sanctions Front

**The Iranian barrel — August 21 is 15 days out.** Sanctions relief permitting dollar-denominated trade in Iranian crude expires then. Iran is front-loading: **2.9M bpd (Jun) → 4.0M (Jul) → 4.2M expected (Aug)**, exports from 1.7M toward **~2.0M bpd**, with **China effectively the only buyer** — non-Chinese refiners have filled August-arrival requirements and the window is too short for their compliance reviews. Enforcement continued *inside* the window (shadow-fleet actions July 29). **The relief is transactional and reversible.**

**The Russian barrel — the discount tells you what this crisis is actually worth.** Urals to India narrowed to **$1–2/bbl against dated Brent**, from **more than $10/bbl in early July**, because Indian refiners bid up Russian grades when Hormuz removed the Middle Eastern alternative. **A reopening re-widens that discount and pushes Urals volume back into competition. The bear case compounds through a second channel.**

**Tariff credibility, in one line:** Washington's **50% tariff on Indian imports takes effect August 27**. India has kept buying Russian crude. The tariff is real; the deterrent is not.
