## The Policy Vector — Watch This Instead of the Missiles

The escalation moved from military to economic overnight. **Trump wants Congress to add tariffs on Iran to the bipartisan Russia/Iran sanctions bill.** Direct effect: near zero — the US imports almost nothing from Iran.

**The live question is the separate 25% secondary tariff on countries trading with Iran**, announced back in January with still no published implementation guidance. That matters because **Iran exports ~1.3M bpd of crude, almost all to China**, plus refined products to the UAE, Turkey, Iraq, India and Pakistan. Enforced strictly, it removes over a million barrels a day from the market — a bigger supply event than anything the strait has produced in two weeks. Unenforced, it is theatre. The UAE, the largest buyer of Iranian fuel oil, has deep US ties and is unlikely to test it.
