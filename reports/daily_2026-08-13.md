**Today's volume data sharpens that considerably, and not in Iran's favour.** If ~9 mb/d is genuinely transiting under US naval escort, then **Iran's chokehold is substantially weaker than either side's rhetoric implies** — Tehran's threat is being priced at 40% effectiveness, not 85%. That cuts two ways and I am not going to pick the comfortable one:

- **De-escalatory read:** a weakening lever is a lever you trade away sooner.
- **Escalatory read:** a lever losing value is a lever you use harder while it still works — which is the same logic that makes the mid-2027 bypass completion a deadline for Iran.

**What breaks the current equilibrium:** a strike on a Gulf oil field or stabilisation train rather than shipping, or crew deaths on a US-interdicted vessel. Neither happened today.
