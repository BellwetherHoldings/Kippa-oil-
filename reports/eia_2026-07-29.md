## One Input, Two Components — Disclosed

**Geopolitical risk jumped 81.7 → 90.3 on the same draw**, and it is worth being explicit about why. The realized-impact damping applies an inventory reality check: when crude is *building* against a claimed supply crisis, that is evidence the crisis is not biting, and the Hormuz claim gets discounted. With a **draw**, the check returns 1.00 — no discount. Damping moved **0.67 → 0.82** (capacity 0.82 × inventory 1.00).

So this single datapoint moved the composite through two paths. **The magnitude is small and measurable:** the geopolitical leg contributed roughly **+0.015** of the +0.20 total move; inventory itself contributed **+0.195**. The double-use is real, it is by design, and it is not what drove today's flip.
