## The Misdated Source — worth recording

The search summary asserted, for "the EIA Weekly Petroleum Status Report released on August 12, 2026 (for the week ending August 7, 2026)":

- Crude inventories **411.7 million barrels**, ~6% below the five-year average
- Commercial crude ex-SPR **increased by 2.0 million barrels**
- Imports **5.8 mb/d**, +117 kb/d

Cross-checked against the platform's own series:

| Claimed as "Aug 7" | Our 2026-07-17 row |
|---|---|
| 411.7 million bbl | **411,675 kbbl** |
| +2.0 million bbl | **+2,010 kbbl** |

**Exact match on both figures.** Whatever that summary was reading, it was the July 17 print.

**Why this is logged rather than shrugged off:** a stale figure presented with a current date is worse than no figure. Had I written this report from it, I would have published a **+2.0 mb build** as this week's news when the platform's actual last print was a **+2.5 mb build on July 31** — and the inventory component carries **30.9% of effective weight**, the heaviest on the board. The cross-check took one grep. **This is now a standing step: no press inventory figure goes into a report without being matched against `data/crude_inventories.csv` first.**
