## 4. The Economic / Sanctions Front

Unchanged from Friday, and the read stands:

- Iran exports **~1.3M bpd, ~90% to China**, at discounts **often exceeding $10/bbl to Brent**, settled in renminbi outside the dollar system.

**New wrinkle:** the Russia refinery campaign cuts the other way on this file. Discounted Russian barrels into China have historically **crowded out Iranian crude**. If Russian refining is 30% down and Moscow exports more crude rather than product, that pressure intensifies — and it makes squeezing Iran alone even less effective.
